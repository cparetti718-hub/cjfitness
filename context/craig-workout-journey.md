### Daily add-on layer (added Oct 27, 2026)

Craig asked to do more without overworking muscles.

**Principle:** train every day without training the same muscle HARD every day — big muscles get ~48h between hard sessions (the plan above handles that); this layer is only work that recovers in ~a day, or submaximal work that creates no damage.

- **Daily vitamins (~10 min, any day incl. rest days):** rotate 2–3 of:
  - Core: dead bug 2 × 10/side or plank 2 × 45s
  - Single-leg calf raise holding a 15: 2 × 15/side (skip on Tuesdays, leg day covers calves)
  - Grip: farmer's hold 2 × 30–45s or wrist curl 2 × 15
- **Grease-the-groove push-ups:** ~half-max sets, 4–6 spread through the day, always 2+ reps shy of failure. Skip Mon and Sun (chest trained hard those days)
- **Optional Fri/Sat light session** (~20 min, only on whichever of Fri/Sat is NOT the going-out night): Lateral Raise / Bicep Curl / Overhead Triceps Extension, 2 × 15 each, 3+ reps shy of failure

**Guardrails for the layer:**
- Eat back the extra burn (~150–250 kcal/day). The surplus is the priority at ~145 lb; extra work that eats the surplus works against the goal
- **Stall rule / cut order:** if the main Mon/Wed/Sun session numbers stall two straight sessions, cut in this order: (1) Fri/Sat light session, (2) grease-the-groove, (3) Thursday finisher. The daily vitamins go last (lightest work)

### Progression rules (the whole game with fixed weight)

- Hit the top of the rep range on EVERY set with clean form → next session, add reps or a set
- When 4 sets feel easy → slow the lowering phase to 3 seconds
- Every 4th week → deload: same exercises, 2 sets each, easy effort
- The log is the gauge: beat last week's numbers

### Form cues on file

- **Presses:** no arching/heaving — if heaving, slow down
- **Rows:** elbow past the torso, squeeze the shoulder blade
- **Upright row:** wide grip, chest height only, elbows below shoulders
- **Y-raise:** thumbs up, arms in a Y, squeeze shoulder blades together
- **Shrug:** straight up to the ears, pause at top, no rolling
- **Lateral raise:** slight elbow bend, stop at shoulder height
- **Curls:** elbows pinned, no swinging
